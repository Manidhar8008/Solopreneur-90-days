# Business Models

Do not choose a model because it sounds exciting. Choose it because it fits your capabilities, customer, distribution, capital, and desired operating leverage.

| Model | Speed to first sale | Capital | Main leverage |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Freelance | High | Low | Skill |
| Consulting | High | Low | Expertise |
| Productized service | High | Low | Process |
| Agency | Medium | Medium | Team/process |
| SaaS | Low | Medium | Software |
| AI product | Medium | Medium | AI + software |
| Digital product | Medium | Low | Knowledge/distribution |
| Subscription | Medium | Medium | Recurring value |
| Marketplace | Low | Medium/High | Network effects |

## Selection rule

Start with the model that gets you **closest to customer evidence with the least irreversible cost**.

Then move toward more leverage only when customer demand supports the move.
