# 08 — Build Your Company

A company is more than a logo and a website.

You need:

- a customer
- an offer
- delivery
- acquisition
- cash flow
- systems
- records
- repeatability

Then decide what the next form should be:

```text
freelance
consulting
productized service
agency
software
vertical AI
startup
partnership
```

A 12-month company plan based on evidence from the previous 90 days.
