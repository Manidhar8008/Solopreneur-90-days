# Verticals

A vertical is a market where the customer language, workflow, compliance, economics, and pain are specific enough to produce a focused solution.

## Research map

| Vertical | Typical buyers | Possible gaps to investigate |
|---|---|---|
| Education | schools, institutes, educators | admissions, communication, content, administration |
| Healthcare | clinics, providers | operations, communication, scheduling, documentation |
| Real estate | agents, developers | lead management, qualification, follow-up |
| Manufacturing | plants, vendors | workflow, quality, reporting, procurement |
| Construction | contractors, suppliers | coordination, quoting, documentation |
| Logistics | fleets, operators | dispatch, tracking, communication |
| Finance | firms, teams | reporting, reconciliation, collections |
| Legal | firms | intake, document workflows, knowledge systems |
| Retail | stores, brands | CRM, inventory, customer engagement |
| Hospitality | hotels, restaurants | reservations, guest operations, retention |
| Local business | SMB owners | lead capture, CRM, WhatsApp, automation |

This is an exploration map, not a list of guaranteed opportunities. Validate each market directly.
