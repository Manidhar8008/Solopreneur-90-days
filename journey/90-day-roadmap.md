# 90-Day Roadmap

## Rules

- One vertical at a time.
- One primary customer.
- Ship weekly.
- Talk to customers every week.
- Track evidence, not motivation.
- Do not build a large product before validating the problem.

## Week 1 — Founder Baseline

**Output:** Founder Profile + constraint map.

- Audit occupation.
- List skills and domain knowledge.
- Track weekly energy and available time.
- Identify current network and audience.
- Identify money constraints.
- Identify technical gaps.

## Week 2 — Market Discovery

**Output:** Vertical thesis.

- Pick 3 verticals.
- Score market access, pain, competition, ability to deliver, and willingness to pay.
- Speak to at least 5 people.
- Document recurring problems.

## Week 3 — Business Model

**Output:** Business Model Decision.

Compare freelance, consulting, productized service, agency, SaaS, AI product, subscription, digital product, and other relevant models.

## Week 4 — Offer

**Output:** One-page offer.

Define buyer, pain, outcome, mechanism, scope, price, proof, and CTA.

## Week 5 — Build

**Output:** Working version 0.1.

Build only the smallest system needed to demonstrate value.

## Week 6 — Customer Acquisition

**Output:** 50–100 target accounts or equivalent pipeline.

Create acquisition lists and test at least two channels.

## Week 7 — Sales

**Output:** Customer conversations + proposals.

Run discovery calls, demos, objections, proposals, and follow-up.

## Week 8 — First Customer

**Output:** First transaction.

Paid is strongest evidence. If payment is not possible yet, secure a committed pilot with an explicit success criterion.

## Week 9 — Delivery

**Output:** Customer result.

Deliver, measure baseline vs result, capture testimonial or evidence where appropriate.

## Week 10 — Productize

**Output:** Repeatable delivery system.

Write SOPs, templates, checklists, automation, onboarding, and reporting.

## Week 11 — Economics

**Output:** Unit economics model.

Track price, delivery hours, acquisition cost, gross margin, retention, expansion, and capacity.

## Week 12 — Scale System

**Output:** Repeatable acquisition + delivery model.

Remove founder bottlenecks where possible.

## Week 13 — Founder Review

**Output:** 12-month plan.

Choose one:

- continue as solopreneur;
- create a company;
- productize further;
- build software;
- seek partnership;
- apply for MW.AI opportunities;
- propose a funding case;
- return to employment with a stronger market position.

## Completion standard

You are not “done” because 90 days passed.

You are done when you have **evidence of market understanding and a repeatable next step**.
