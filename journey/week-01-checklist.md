# Week 1 — Occupation to Market

- [ ] Document current occupation.
- [ ] List 10 skills you already use.
- [ ] List 5 things your employer does for customers.
- [ ] Identify 3 customer types.
- [ ] Map one customer workflow end-to-end.
- [ ] List 10 recurring customer problems.
- [ ] Score problems by frequency, cost, urgency, and accessibility.
- [ ] Interview at least 3 people.
- [ ] Select one market gap to investigate.
- [ ] Write your first problem hypothesis.

**Deliverable:** one occupation-to-market map + one evidence-backed problem hypothesis.
