# Customer Interview Script

## Objective

Understand the customer's current workflow before proposing a solution.

## Questions

1. Walk me through how you currently handle the problem.
2. What happens immediately before this?
3. What happens immediately after this?
4. How often does it occur?
5. Who is involved?
6. What does the current process cost in time or money?
7. What happens when it goes wrong?
8. What have you already tried?
9. What would a successful outcome look like?
10. If this problem disappeared tomorrow, what would become possible?

## Do not ask

> Would you buy my product?

Ask about **existing behavior, existing spending, existing pain, and measurable outcomes**.

## Evidence

- Current workflow
- Existing solution
- Frequency
- Cost
- Decision maker
- Urgency
- Existing budget
- Desired outcome
- Exact customer language
