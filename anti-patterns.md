# 10 Anti-Patterns That Kill a 90-Day Sprint

1. Building before talking to customers.
2. Choosing technology before choosing the problem.
3. Targeting everyone.
4. Confusing attention with demand.
5. Learning endlessly without shipping.
6. Pricing from personal fear instead of customer value.
7. Measuring activity instead of evidence.
8. Automating before proving the workflow manually.
9. Starting five products simultaneously.
10. Treating a job title as the limit of your economic capability.

**Narrow the problem. Talk to the market. Ship. Measure. Repeat.**
