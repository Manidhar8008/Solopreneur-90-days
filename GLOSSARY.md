# Glossary

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Solopreneur | A person who operates an independent business using systems, tools, automation, and external collaborators without requiring a traditional large organization. |
| Occupation Audit | An analysis of your current work, skills, customer exposure, assets, constraints, and missing capabilities. |
| Value Chain | The sequence of activities through which a company creates and delivers customer value. |
| Market Gap | A meaningful customer need that is underserved, inefficiently served, or poorly solved. |
| Capability Gap | A skill, system, technology, distribution channel, or business capability needed to address a market gap. |
| Vertical | A specific industry or customer domain. |
| Vertical-first | Starting with a narrow customer domain and learning its workflows, economics, language, and problems deeply. |
| Productized Service | A service converted into a standardized offer with defined scope, outcome, price, and delivery process. |
| ROI | The measurable economic value a customer receives relative to the cost of the solution. |
| Founder OS | The repeatable operating system used to make decisions, acquire customers, build products, measure results, and improve. |
| Evidence | Observable proof such as interviews, proposals, sales, revenue, customer outcomes, shipped software, or measurable improvement. |
| MW.AI | The practical ecosystem referenced by this curriculum for studying vertical AI, automation, products, and founder opportunities. |
