# 04 — Businesses Disappear

**EVIDENCE**

U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics data show that only 34.7% of establishments born in 2013 were still operating in 2023. This is establishment-level data, not a statistic specifically about solopreneurs. [BLS](https://www.bls.gov/opub/ted/2024/business-survival-after-10-years.htm)

**INFERENCE**

Treat survival as an operating problem.

The answer is not fear. The answer is:

- test demand early;
- keep fixed costs low;
- maintain cash visibility;
- avoid unnecessary complexity;
- build repeatable acquisition;
- adapt when evidence changes.

Do not use generic survival statistics as a prophecy for your own business.
