# 06 — Sales Is Not a Personality Trait

**PRACTICAL TRUTH**

The early solopreneur often has to perform sales personally.

That means:

- asking uncomfortable questions;
- hearing "no";
- following up;
- negotiating;
- collecting money;
- clarifying scope;
- protecting margins.

You do not need to become a loud salesperson.

You need a repeatable process for finding people with a problem and helping them make a decision.
