# 10 — Your Job Can Be a Paid Apprenticeship

**PRACTICAL TRUTH**

A job can expose you to:

- customers;
- workflows;
- industry language;
- buying decisions;
- recurring problems;
- software;
- compliance;
- operational bottlenecks.

Use that exposure ethically.

Do not steal employer intellectual property, confidential information, customer lists, or proprietary material.

Learn the market. Create your own knowledge and assets.
