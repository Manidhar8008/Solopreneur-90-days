# 07 — Income Can Be Lumpy

**PRACTICAL TRUTH**

A job normally converts time into predictable payroll.

A solopreneur business converts a system of:

**acquisition + offer + delivery + collection + retention**

into owner income.

The result may be uneven.

Plan for:

- slow months;
- delayed payments;
- customer churn;
- pipeline gaps;
- seasonal demand;
- unexpected expenses.

Do not build your personal finances around your best month.
