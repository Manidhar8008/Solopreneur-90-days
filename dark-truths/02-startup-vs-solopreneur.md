# 02 — Do Not Confuse a Fundable Startup With a Good Small Business

**PRACTICAL TRUTH**

A venture-backed startup and a durable one-person business solve different optimization problems.

A startup may optimize for:

- market size
- growth rate
- capital efficiency
- defensibility
- venture returns

A solopreneur may optimize for:

- owner income
- cash flow
- freedom
- small-team leverage
- stability
- control

Your business does not need to look impressive to an investor to be economically useful to you.
