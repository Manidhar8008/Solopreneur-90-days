# 11 — Solopreneurship Is Not Automatically Better

**PRACTICAL TRUTH**

A good career can be a good career.

Choose employment when it gives you:

- learning;
- strong compensation;
- mentorship;
- stability;
- meaningful work;
- access you cannot yet create yourself.

Choose entrepreneurship when the trade-offs make sense for your goals and constraints.

The purpose of this repository is **economic agency**, not ideology.
