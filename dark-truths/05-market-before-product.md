# 05 — A Working Product Can Still Be Worthless

**EVIDENCE**

CB Insights' 2026 analysis of 431 VC-backed companies that shut down since 2023 reports poor product-market fit among the main cited causes and notes that capital running out was often the final cause rather than the root cause. [CB Insights](https://www.cbinsights.com/research/report/startup-failure-reasons-top/)

**PRACTICAL TRUTH**

A product can be:

- technically excellent;
- beautiful;
- AI-powered;
- fast;
- well documented;

and still fail because the customer does not care enough to buy it.

Build evidence before sophistication.
