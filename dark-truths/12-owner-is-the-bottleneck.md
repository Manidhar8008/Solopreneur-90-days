# 12 — You Can Accidentally Build Yourself a New Job

**PRACTICAL TRUTH**

If every sale, support request, delivery task, invoice, and decision requires you forever, you have built self-employment, not a leveraged business.

Watch for:

- too many custom requests;
- too much operational memory;
- no documented delivery;
- no repeatable acquisition;
- no time for improvement.

The long-term goal is not to work alone.

It is to make one person's judgment produce more output through systems.
