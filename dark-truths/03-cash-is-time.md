# 03 — Revenue Is Not Cash, and Profit Is Not Freedom

**EVIDENCE**

The U.S. Small Business Administration explicitly treats cash-flow management as central to operational survival and notes that even profitable businesses can struggle when cash is poorly managed. [SBA — Cash Flow Strategy](https://www.sba.gov/event/85811)

**PRACTICAL TRUTH**

Watch:

- cash in
- cash out
- payment timing
- receivables
- recurring obligations
- taxes
- personal withdrawals

A $10,000 invoice you cannot collect is not the same thing as $10,000 in available cash.
