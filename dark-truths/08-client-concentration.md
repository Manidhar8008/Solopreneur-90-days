# 08 — One Big Customer Can Be a Hidden Dependency

**PRACTICAL TRUTH**

One customer providing most of your revenue feels safe until that customer changes budget, leadership, priorities, or vendor strategy.

Track customer concentration.

A successful solopreneur should gradually build:

- diversified demand;
- transferable skills;
- reusable systems;
- multiple acquisition channels.

Do not confuse one large contract with a resilient company.
