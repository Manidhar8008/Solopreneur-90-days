# Revenue Math

Revenue is a function of customers, price, frequency, and retention.

```text
Revenue = Customers × Average Revenue per Customer
```

For recurring revenue:

```text
ARR = Active Customers × Average Monthly Revenue × 12
```

## $1M annual examples

```text
1,000 customers × $1,000/year = $1M
100 customers × $10,000/year = $1M
20 customers × $50,000/year = $1M
10 customers × $100,000/year = $1M
```

The point is not to choose a number first. The point is to understand the operating consequences of the chosen model.

Ask:

- How many buyers exist?
- How do I reach them?
- How long does acquisition take?
- How many hours does delivery require?
- What is gross margin?
- What can become software or automation?
