# Founder OS

The Founder OS is a practical system for converting scattered work into a repeatable business engine.

## Layers

```text
Identity
  ↓
Market
  ↓
Problem
  ↓
Offer
  ↓
Acquisition
  ↓
Delivery
  ↓
Measurement
  ↓
Automation
  ↓
Capital / Team / Partnerships
```

## Weekly review

- What did I learn from the market?
- What did I ship?
- How many customer conversations happened?
- What did people pay for?
- Where did delivery consume time?
- Which work can be automated?
- Which skill is now the bottleneck?
- What should I stop doing?

## Founder maturity

**Level 0:** Skilled worker

**Level 1:** Independent operator

**Level 2:** Customer-facing specialist

**Level 3:** Productized operator

**Level 4:** System builder

**Level 5:** Company builder
