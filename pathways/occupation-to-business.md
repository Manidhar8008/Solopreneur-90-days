# Occupation → Business Mapper

## The question

> What is valuable about your current occupation that can be converted into an independent offer?

## Step 1 — Map the job

Write down:

- tasks you perform;
- decisions you make;
- software you use;
- data you handle;
- customers you interact with;
- problems that repeatedly occur;
- reports you create;
- delays and bottlenecks;
- expensive mistakes;
- work customers hate doing.

## Step 2 — Map the company

Ask:

- What does the company sell?
- Who pays?
- Why do they pay?
- Which activities create the most value?
- Which activities are outsourced?
- Which activities are still manual?
- Where does the company mark up value?

## Step 3 — Find the gap

The target is not “copy my employer”.

The target is a **focused capability gap**.

```text
Company does 100 things
        ↓
Choose one valuable sub-problem
        ↓
Learn enough to solve it end-to-end
        ↓
Offer it directly to a buyer
```

## Step 4 — Choose your leverage

Add one or more:

- AI
- automation
- software
- data
- distribution
- niche expertise
- reusable processes
- content
- partnerships

## Example transformation

```text
O2C / collections professional
        ↓
Understands collections workflow
        ↓
Finds recurring manual reporting gap
        ↓
Learns automation + AI + CRM
        ↓
Builds receivables workflow product
        ↓
Targets SMB finance teams
        ↓
Charges for outcome / workflow
```

The exact business is validated by customers, not by the framework.
