# Student → Solopreneur

Use learning time to build a small commercial capability, portfolio, customer evidence, and network.

## Skill stack

1. Market understanding
2. Customer discovery
3. Offer design
4. Acquisition
5. Delivery
6. Automation
7. Economics
8. Productization

## 90-day objective

Create one narrow offer, validate it with real people, and build evidence that can support an independent business or a stronger career move.

## Exit evidence

- One chosen market
- One customer profile
- One offer
- One shipped asset
- Customer conversations
- Sales evidence
- Case study or result
- 12-month next-step plan
