# MW.AI Apprenticeship Layer

MW.AI provides a practical ecosystem for studying how technical systems become vertical business products.

## Product laboratories

### Vasuki
Study local-first personal intelligence, multimodal context, memory, orchestration, and automation concepts.

### Janani.ai
Study personal intelligence and discipline-oriented product thinking.

### Jan.ai
Study media management and information workflows.

### Ani.ai
Study creative workflow, memory, and writing-assistance concepts.

### AIM1000
Study job acquisition as an automation and systems problem.

### MW.AI
Study vertical AI/business systems and how the same underlying intelligence layer can be adapted to different industries.

### Founder Rooms
Study founder-centric product design, AI CRM concepts, collaboration, business intelligence, and local founder ecosystems.

## Opportunity ladder

A learner who demonstrates strong execution may eventually be considered for:

1. Open-source contribution
2. Apprenticeship / internship work
3. Paid project work
4. Employment
5. Product collaboration
6. Founder partnership
7. Funding discussion

All decisions remain evidence-based and opportunity-dependent.
