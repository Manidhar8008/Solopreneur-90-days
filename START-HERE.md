# 🚪 Start Here

## Solopreneur 90 Days — in plain English

You do **not** need to be a developer.

You do **not** need a startup idea.

You do **not** need to quit your job.

You need a willingness to learn how value is created and test that value with real customers.

## The simple system

```text
01. Know yourself
        ↓
02. Find a problem
        ↓
03. Find a customer
        ↓
04. Create an offer
        ↓
05. Get paid
        ↓
06. Deliver results
        ↓
07. Build your system
        ↓
08. Build your company
```

## Your first action

Open the [Occupation → Market Gap Audit](templates/occupation-market-gap-audit.md).

Write down:

1. What you do now.
2. What you know.
3. Who you understand.
4. What your employer or market sells.
5. What customers still struggle with.
6. What you are missing.
7. What you could learn.
8. What someone might pay you to solve.

**Do not build software yet.**

First understand the customer.

## The rule for the entire repository

```text
Customer problem
      ↓
Manual solution
      ↓
Simple tool
      ↓
Automation
      ↓
AI / software only when useful
```

Technology is leverage. It is not the starting point.

## Your 90-day target

Do not optimize for a certificate.

Produce evidence:

**customer conversations → useful offer → first payment → customer result → repeatable process**

The $1M model is a long-term revenue architecture, not a 90-day promise.
