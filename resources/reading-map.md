# Reading & Learning Map

Use this as a map, not a requirement to consume endless content.

## Business

Study business models, positioning, pricing, sales, customer development, unit economics, and operations.

## Technology

Study only the technology needed to ship the chosen solution.

## Founder skill

Practice written communication, customer interviews, proposal writing, negotiation, project management, and documentation.

## The rule

Every research session should produce one of:

- a decision;
- a customer question;
- a build task;
- an experiment;
- a reusable artifact.
