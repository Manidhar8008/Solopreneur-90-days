# Reality Check Before You Start

Ask yourself:

- Can I tolerate uncertainty?
- Can I talk to strangers?
- Can I sell without immediate validation?
- Can I manage money?
- Can I work without external deadlines?
- Can I keep learning after rejection?
- Can I deliver when the work becomes boring?
- Can I say no to bad customers?
- Can I keep a pipeline while serving current customers?
- Can I maintain a cash buffer?

If many answers are "no", that does not disqualify you.

It tells you what to learn first.
