# 05 — Get Paid

Revenue is evidence.

Do not confuse:

- compliments with demand;
- followers with customers;
- demos with sales;
- proposals with cash;
- revenue with profit;
- profit with owner income.

Learn:

- pricing
- proposals
- negotiation
- deposits
- contracts
- invoicing
- payment collection
- cash-flow tracking

### Output

Your first paid transaction, or clear evidence about why the current offer did not sell.
